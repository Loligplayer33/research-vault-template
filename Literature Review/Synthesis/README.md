## Suggested Structure for Each Note

Every synthesis note should generally use the same shape:

- **Intro paragraph** — what the note is for, what tensions to watch for
- **Current Argument**
  - **Working Thoughts** — your thinking-in-progress, paper-by-paper. Reactions, positions-in-development, ideas being tested. Scratch material.
  - **Synthesized Position** — draft-quality prose with inline citekeys. Fills in once multiple papers back a claim. Eventually becomes project draft material.
- **Paper Contributions** — lightweight log of what each paper contributes. Each paper is an H3 subheading wikilinked to its main paper note with the citekey as alias.
- **Related** — wikilinks to the other synthesis notes and to [[Literature Review/Overview Synthesis and Reading Map]]

## The Discipline

After finishing each paper:

1. Open the 1–3 synthesis notes the paper touches.
2. Add 2–6 bullets under an H3 for that paper (or extend existing bullets).
3. If the paper shifts your thinking, update Working Thoughts.
4. When multiple papers back a claim, promote it into Synthesized Position as cited prose.

Keep bullets short (1–2 sentences). Use plain citekeys `(smith2024)` in prose. H3 wikilinks handle backlink coverage.

For AI-assisted integration after a paper is read and Zotero notes are complete, use [[ai/synthesis-integration-workflow]]. That workflow creates a verdict-scaled checklist in `ai/outputs/` and preserves the synthesis notes as user-owned argument space.
